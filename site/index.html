Duckish does not beg for draws.

It accepts one only when it is the smaller bird. Otherwise it plays on.

Duckish is a chess engine written in Rust. It runs on an i5 laptop with four threads and 256 MB of hash. It is rated around 2860 at rapid on Lichess.

With the black pieces it plays defensively. It gives up space early and waits for counterplay. Against a stronger opponent it is happy to take a draw.

With the white pieces it plays normally. It builds slowly and presses when the position allows. It never offers a draw. It accepts one only when it is the lower rated side.

As white it likes the Reti. As black it answers 1 e4 with the Sicilian and 1 d4 with a Grunfeld setup.
